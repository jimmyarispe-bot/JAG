THE STAGES NOW HAVE THREE GROUPS. pipeline is the thirteen numbered steps
of the live flow and is all a card's dropdown offers. closed is the three
Old Students columns. retired is Records Requested, Admissions Review and
Assessment Scheduled - offered nowhere, kept so the type and any stored
value survive, and so assessment can come back when it is built.

Every stage stays in LEAD_STAGES. A stage removed from that array is a
stage leadStageLabel cannot name, and the registry's own comment records
what that costs: Julian Oubre Towa vanished from the board in September
because one stage was missing from one list.

TWO RENAMES, NOT TWO NEW STAGES.

  waitlisted     -> "Decision Needed"
  not_returning  -> "Alumni"

Sedona Simeonov has been on waitlisted since 25 August and nobody has
told her mother anything, because the waitlist letter is switched on and

her forty-six days; a new stage would have meant moving her by hand.

Thirteen children were "Not Returning", which is the one thing that never

A third mismatch fixed in passing - the board said "Declined to Enroll"
