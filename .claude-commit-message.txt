feat(teacher): Submit at the top of the week as well as the foot

PAYSHEET CHANGE 1 of 6, from Jimmy on 8 October 2026:

    "submit buttons located at top n bottom"

The week is a long page - thirteen classes for Renee Tracewell last week,
each with a register under it, then the extras, then the hours, then the
note - and the only Submit was at the very bottom of all of it.

ONE HANDLER, TWO BUTTONS, AND THAT IS THE POINT

Both buttons call the same `submitWeek`, and both are disabled by the same
`pending`.

Two buttons wired to two copies of the same call is how a week gets
submitted twice. A teacher presses the top one; nothing visibly moves,
because the page does not scroll and the transition takes a moment; she
scrolls down and presses the other. The second call then runs against a week
that is already closed and answers with an error about her own submission -
a confusing way to end a Friday, and one she would reasonably read as the
JAG having lost her work.

The note is read AT PRESS TIME rather than captured when the handler is
made, so the top button sends whatever she has typed into the box at the
bottom.

The top button carries the same running total as the bottom one. Somebody
submitting from the top should see the figure she is agreeing to without
scrolling to find it.

to any teacher, precisely because the campus-scoped policy was wrong for
this one question. Peter Alouise, 2 October: "Most of my kids are not on the

is the same list either way.
