four money letters stop reaching the school leader

Jimmy, 10 October, reviewing the six letters that fire while a family is
filling in the application. On the two state-funding verifications:

  "5 n 6 are always me"

He does the funding verification, not the campus. Which surfaced what
the blind copy shipped the night before was actually doing: every family
letter on the email channel is copied to that campus's school leader,
and four of these six are money -

  state_funding_needed_email        carries an Award Amount line
  funding_approved_email
  funding_rejected_email
  financial_aid_requested_email

So Nina and Heather were receiving blind copies of funding mail. Against
a rule that has not moved since September: "remember only danni n me see
anything related to money." Asked whether to take them out: "yes".

NOTHING LEAKED. award_amount renders empty for a family who has not sent
it, and that is the only kind of family the letter reaches. The line was
in the letter and the copy was working, which is enough.

BY TEMPLATE KEY. A category can be re-pointed in the template editor by
somebody who has never heard of this rule; a key is the letter itself. A
new money letter has to be added to the set by hand, which is the right
amount of friction for a list whose only job is keeping four addresses
out of a mailbox.

The other two of the six - the portal welcome and the missing-documents
chaser - keep their blind copy. They are not money and the school leader
should see them.
