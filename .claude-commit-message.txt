/dashboard/finance/teacher-pay/monthly, linked from the weekly screen.

WHO SEES IT. mayReadPayroll, the same gate as the weekly screen, which asks
may_read_all_teacher_pay() rather than re-deciding in TypeScript. Jimmy and
Danni. A refusal says so in words: an empty page would read as "nothing was
paid", which is the one wrong answer this page must never give.

"PAID OUT" IS DELIBERATELY NARROW

ONLY APPROVED WEEKS. A submitted week is a claim; an approved one is a
decision. A total that counted submissions would move every time a teacher
pressed Submit, and would overstate every month in the days between Friday
and whenever Jimmy gets to it - a figure nobody could reconcile against a
bank statement.

Weeks still waiting are listed SEPARATELY AND BY NAME. Folding them in would
be wrong and dropping them silently would be worse: a month nobody has got
to would read as a month that is finished.

A WEEK BELONGS TO THE MONTH ITS MONDAY FALLS IN

29 September runs into October. Splitting it would mean apportioning one
frozen figure by day, which is a second arithmetic nobody asked for and
nobody could check. Said on the page as well as here, because the other
choice is defensible and somebody will wonder which was made.

THE CAMPUS COLUMNS AND THE PAID TOTAL COME FROM DIFFERENT PLACES

This is the part worth reading.

WHAT WAS PAID is the override if there is one, otherwise the week's total.
WHERE THE WORK WAS is the campus split of the same week.

They can disagree, because an override is a decision about a WHOLE WEEK and
nothing records which campus it was meant for. Apportioning it would be
putting a number in Jimmy's mouth.

So the campus figures always sum to the COMPUTED total, the paid figure is
what actually went out, and any difference is shown as its own line with the
reason beside the teacher's name - never quietly reconciled. A month that
was corrected shows both numbers and why.

Each teacher's row carries the same: the paid figure, and the computed one
underneath it whenever they differ.

ONE LOAD PER APPROVED WEEK, through loadTeacherWeek - the same function the
weekly screen uses. Thirteen teachers across five Mondays is sixty-five
small reads rather than one query nobody can follow, and it means the two
screens cannot disagree about what a week was worth.

Eastern for "this month", because the school runs on Eastern and a month
that rolls over at 7pm for somebody in California is a month nobody can
reconcile.
