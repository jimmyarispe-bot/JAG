We do not do interviews, and the family is told what they actually submitted

THE WORD. On 17 September a shadow day was booked for Maddox Mixon at The
Academy HS and his mother was emailed an interview confirmation telling her to
prepare report cards and IEP summaries. Heather Badger-Brown, who had
scheduled no interview and conducts none: "The Jag is setting up interviews.
We haven't interviewed prospective families. It didn't invite me either!"
Jimmy: "there shouldn't be anything that says interview. we don't do
interviews."

"Interest meeting" now, everywhere a person reads it: the booking dialog, the
stage on every card's dropdown, the case profile, the staff panel, and the
chip a FAMILY sees on their own application. The internal names are untouched
- admissions_interviews, interview_type, the interview_scheduled stage key -
because renaming those is a migration across the whole pipeline for a word
nobody outside the code reads.

THE BEHAVIOUR. scheduleInterview read `interview_type` to choose the stage and
then sent the interview letter whatever had been booked. A shadow day is
stored as an interview of type initial_assessment, so the type was already on
the record and already being read. It decides the message now too.

THE DATE reads "Thursday, 24 September 2026 at 1:00 PM", in Eastern, instead
of "9/24/2026, 1:00:00 PM" - seconds on a school appointment, rendered in
whatever timezone the server happened to be in rather than the one the school
is in.

THE THANK-YOU PAGE KNEW AND DIDN'T SAY. The invited-application route has
passed `applied=1` to /apply/thank-you since it shipped, and the page never
read it - so a family who had just completed a full application was told
"Inquiry Received" and invited to book a tour they had already had. Both
notices are now Jimmy's own words. The inquiry one is unchanged, deliberately:
"this needs to be the inquiry received confirmation notice." The application
one names the child, using their preferred name where the family gave one.

One correction to his application text, which he was told about: he wrote
"your will receive". It reads "you will receive".

MIGRATIONS 415 AND 416 ARE ALREADY RUN. They carry the acceptance letter, the
shadow-days invitation and the staff notices, all in his wording. Two of those
templates - shadow_day_scheduled and staff_shadow_day_scheduled - have been
sitting unused since they were created, because the code that fires them is in
this commit.

staff_interview_scheduled had been dispatched on every booking since the
feature shipped with no template behind it, so the engine found nothing and
sent nothing, silently. That is why Heather heard nothing, and it is fixed in
the database rather than here.

Still open, named so it is not rediscovered a third time: five staff templates
sit on the internal_note channel, which records a message and never sends one,
including staff_new_inquiry - so nobody is emailed when a family inquires.

Verified in order: typecheck, the full build, then the admissions suite.
