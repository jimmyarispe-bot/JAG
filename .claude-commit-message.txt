The application comes before the shadow days, and the decision after them

The pipeline board carried a stage order nobody at The Academy Way works to:
shadow days before the application, then two columns - Documents Pending and
Committee Review - that arrived with a generic admissions template. Jimmy,
24 September: "the application started comes before the shadow days scheduled.
after the shadow days completed, then the decision needs to be made." And on
the other two: "documents are included in the application so why would there
be a documents pending column. we don't have a committee so where did that
come from."

The board is now the real sequence, and it matches the order the emails have
always fired in: invited to apply after the tour or interest meeting, invited
to shadow days once the application is submitted, accepted or denied once the
shadow days are done.

  ... Tour Conducted
    -> Application Started
    -> Application Submitted
    -> Shadow Days Scheduled
    -> Shadow Days Completed
    -> Decision

DECISION IS NOT A STAGE. A family in that column is still at
shadow_day_completed; what changed is that a question is open on them. The
column is drawn from the open accept-or-deny gates, so it fills and empties by
itself and nobody has to move a card into it. Inventing a stage that only
enters when told to is how a pipeline starts lying about where a child is.

Those families are removed from Shadow Days Completed while they stand in
Decision - two cards for one child means clearing the board twice, each pass
believing the other had not happened. The column also obeys the filters: a
board narrowed to one campus does not leave another campus's decisions
standing in the last column.

The wording on the card comes from GATES, the same object the Decisions page
and the server action read, so the question a school leader sees and the
consequence of their answer cannot drift apart.

A failed gate read leaves the column undrawn rather than drawn empty. "Nobody
is waiting" and "I could not find out" are different answers and must not look
the same.

Documents Pending and Committee Review are deactivated, not deleted. Their
stage keys stay valid so a lead carrying records_requested or
admissions_review still resolves to something; deleting the definitions would
make such a lead match no column and vanish without an error - the exact bug
documented on shadow_day_completed in the same file. Verified empty first:
zero leads in either
(supabase/diagnostics/who_is_in_the_two_columns_2026_09_24.sql).
