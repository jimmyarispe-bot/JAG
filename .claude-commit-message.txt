the last gate can finally be answered

Jimmy, the night of 13 October, having booked shadow days for 1:30am and
waited: "i didn't receive any followup email with decision to accept or send
denial letter."

He was right, and it was worse than a missing email. The booking scan had
recognised a shadow day by its title since 11 October and recorded NOTHING -
there was nowhere to put it, deliberately, because on 10 October one had gone
into both admissions_tours AND the interviews table, which is two wrong answers
rather than none. So nothing ever wrote shadow_day_completed, and accept_or_deny
- the gate that opens behind that stage - had never opened once in the life of
this platform. Eight children sat at Shadow Day Scheduled with nothing behind
them.

WHAT NOW EXISTS, END TO END

  the family books              already worked - same calendar, same scan
  the JAG records it            NEW - admissions_shadow_days, migration 550
  the letter at the END         NEW - staff_shadow_day_held, migration 552
  she presses Accept or Deny    NEW - the post-call page's third occasion
  shadow_day_completed written  NEW - by something other than a bulk import
  the decision runs             the SAME code the gate runs

THE LETTER FIRES AT ends_at, NOT AT THE START

Jimmy, 14 October: "receipt/delivery of the shadow day letter doesn't need to
fire until the end of the calendar appt time/day. not the beginning like with
the others". Every other notes letter in this platform is timed to an
appointment's start. This one is not, because it opens "Shadow Days completed
for ..." and asks her to accept or deny, and neither is answerable while the
child is still in the building. So ends_at is a column on the row rather than
something recomputed, and the queue row's sendAt is that column.

THE TWO BUTTONS ARE HIS WORDS

"let's make the buttons this - accept-send email, deny-send email". Used
literally, hyphen included. Accept first, as the mockup reads.

Both run applyAdmissionsDecision - which is submitAdmissionsDecision's body,
moved to decisions-core.ts word for word. TWO DOORS ONTO ONE DECISION MUST NOT
DO TWO DIFFERENT THINGS: an acceptance made on a phone at the school gate and
one made on the decisions board are the same event, same decision row, same
approved acceptance letter, same enrollment packet, same audit. The move was
needed because the page behind a letter has no signed-in session for
assertAnyPermission to read. decisions.ts keeps the permission check and the
FormData and delegates; nothing about an acceptance changed.

THE DENY BUTTON DOES NOT SEND, AND SAYS SO

There is one approved decline letter in the JAG - application_declined_email -
and it is keyed to shadow_days_not_invited, a refusal to invite rather than a
refusal after the days happened. Firing it here would tell a family, and the
record, that they were refused shadow days they had just attended.

So the denial records the decision, marks the days held, moves the child to
Declined, and sends nothing. The button's own consequence line says that, the
page afterwards says it again, and a test asserts both so nobody quietly
rewords it before the letter exists. Jimmy's call, not mine.

THE TRIPWIRE FIRED AGAIN, AS DESIGNED

tour-notes-step.test.ts asserts the complete list of button values, because
recordPostCallDecision writes the school leader's notes FIRST and that write
meets lead_call_outcomes' CHECK constraint before anything else. On 13 October
the tour's eleventh button was missing from that constraint and the first live
test died on the first statement. This time the test failed in one second,
before any test, and migration 553 is the constraint catching up.

WHAT THE SCAN NOW DOES WITH A SHADOW DAY

  dedupes by (lead, start time) - the tours table learned that one the hard way
  writes ends_at, day_count (2 at GA/FL, 1 at HS/Virtual) and the type
  moves the stage ONLY from interest_meeting_held or application_submitted,
    because "shadow day for virtual n hs happen before the application" - so a
    child arrives here from two different places and must never be dragged back
  queues the notes letter at ends_at, same one-day cutoff as the tour
