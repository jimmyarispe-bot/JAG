Acceptance hands the family to the business office, and says so

Three things were wrong with what happens after a school leader clicks Accept.

THE FAMILY'S LETTER GAVE THEM HOMEWORK. It listed four "enrollment steps",
three of which render fixed sentences saying details will follow later, and
the fourth of which linked a family with no account to a password box. It was
signed "Admissions" rather than by the person who met their child. Jimmy's
wording replaces it, and it says the one thing a family needs: there is
nothing for you to do until our business office contacts you, and here are
the three things they will finalise.

One change to his text, and he was told: he wrote "welcome him and your
family". Gender is not recorded on a lead - zero of 107 students carry it -
so a fixed "him" reaches a girl's family. The child's first name says the same
thing and cannot be wrong.

NOBODY WAS TOLD A STUDENT HAD BEEN ACCEPTED. The staff template existed and
was is_active, which is exactly why nobody noticed: it sat on the
internal_note channel, which records a message and never sends one. A row
appeared, no mail left the building, no error was raised. Moved to
staff_email, which delivers.

And it reached the wrong people even then. school_admissions_contacts answers
"who at this CAMPUS should be told", which is right for an inquiry and wrong
for an acceptance - that is the moment admissions hands a family to the
business office, and the business office is network-level. Jimmy was a contact
on The Academy FL and on no other campus, so three of four schools could
accept a student in silence.

network-office.ts adds him to the recipients for that ONE event, listed
explicitly. Adding him to every campus's contact row would also have worked,
and would have sent him every inquiry, upload and funding alert at four
schools.

THE ENROLLMENT PACKET WAS NEVER BUILT. submitAdmissionsDecision only generates
the packet, and only stamps the application's status and decision date, when
it is handed an application id. The gate never passed one. So accepting a
student produced no packet at all, while the email told the family to go and
complete one. The gate now reads the lead's most recent application and hands
it across; a lead with no application still accepts, it simply gets no packet,
which is the honest outcome when there is nothing to build one from.

Migration 415 carries both templates and verifies its own work - it raises
rather than returning quietly if either row did not move, because a template
that silently did not change is the failure this commit exists to end.

The decline letter is still absent and still deliberate. Writing one is
Jimmy's to approve, not a migration's to invent.
