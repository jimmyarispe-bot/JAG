The form builder starts by looking, and a class row says which day it was

TWO THINGS, BOTH SMALL, BOTH READ-ONLY.

/dashboard/admin/forms is ship one of four: every section and question in the
live interest form, what type each is, whether it is required, what the
choices are, and - in English rather than as a condition tree - which campus
sees which section. Nothing on it writes. The form is on version 22 because
twenty-two migrations were the only way to change a sentence, and before any
edit button exists somebody has to be able to SEE the form in one place.

It says two things a definition can hide: a section that lists a question the
form does not have, and a question that belongs to no section and is therefore
never asked of anybody. Both are invisible from every other screen.

describeCondition is pure and tested (12 cases). Its rule is that an
unreadable condition prints its raw value rather than nothing, because a
visibility rule that renders as silence reads as "always shown" - the most
dangerous wrong answer that function could give.

Also: the finance timesheet now prints the date under the weekday and time.
"Mon 8:00 AM" is legible while you are looking at one week and useless six
weeks later at a bank statement. The date was already on every row.
