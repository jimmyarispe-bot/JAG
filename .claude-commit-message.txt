skip next stage

Jimmy, 15 October 2026:

  "beginning with the inquiry email and each time a school leader receives an
   email there always needs to be a button that says 'skip next stage' - this
   is in addition to whatever button/decision is already there."

and, asked what he was actually trying to achieve:

  "a parent has complete the inquiry form, and then goes through the other
   stages but the school leader doesn't do the shadow days and just move to
   acceptance. this is just an example but can happen at any stage except the
   inquiry"

── NO NEW LETTER ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Not one, and for an hour I was sure there had to be three. occasionForStage()
in automation/post-call.ts already decides which buttons a school leader is
shown FROM THE CHILD'S STAGE, and all three "Record your notes" letters point
at that one page with that one token. So the leader's question is a function
of where the child is:

  New Inquiry .. Interest Meeting Held   Send the tour request      (GA, FL)
                                         Send the shadow day invite (HS, V)
  Tour Scheduled / Completed             Send the application
  Shadow Days Scheduled / Completed      Accept - send email
                                         Deny - send email

Moving a child one rung up that ladder and re-sending the letter for the rung
she lands on is the whole feature. Every letter it needs is written, approved
and live, and between them they have sent 93 times.

── THE LADDER READS THE STEP NUMBER, NOT THE OCCASION ──────────────────────

occasionForStage('application_submitted') returns 'interest_meeting' - it
names only the tour and shadow-day stages and everything else falls through.
An occasion-driven skip would have taken a child who has toured AND applied
and landed her back at the tour, which is Jimmy's own example arriving at the
wrong end of the flow. So skipTargetForStage reads LEAD_STAGES.step, which is
written down rather than computed and whose own comment says it is written
down for this kind of reason.

  child at steps 1-2    skips the Interest Meeting  -> Interest Meeting Held
  steps 3-4, GA and FL  skips the Tour              -> Tour Completed
  steps 3-4, HS and V   skips the Shadow Days       -> Shadow Days Completed
  steps 5-7             skips the Application       -> Application Submitted
  steps 8-9             skips the Shadow Days       -> Shadow Days Completed
  steps 10-13           nothing. "no skip button here"

A first version landed on the START of the stage after the skipped one, which
quietly skipped TWO stages a press: from New Inquiry it reached Tour Completed
and offered "Send the application" to a family who had seen nothing. Landing
on the COMPLETION of the stage being skipped is what makes one press one
stage. Both faults are in the test by name, because neither shows up as a
failure - only as a school leader being asked the wrong thing.

── THE CARD SAYS THE STAGE WAS SKIPPED ─────────────────────────────────────

admissions_lead_stage_history already records every move and who made it, so
the jump was always auditable. Offered that alone, Jimmy chose otherwise:
"The card says the stage was skipped".

He is right. The stage the Accept/Deny page reads is shadow_day_completed,
which the board labels "Shadow Days Completed" - so a child whose shadow days
were skipped would carry a card claiming days nobody attended, read by people
who were not in the room. 557 adds skipped_stages and both the list and the
record now say so beside the badge rather than instead of it, because the
stage is still where the child is and every gate and filter still reads it.

── NOTHING REACHES THE FAMILY ──────────────────────────────────────────────

Asked what a press should send a parent, Jimmy chose: nothing yet, and the
leader gets the next letter with its own skip button on it. Every letter this
queues is a staff letter. No family hears anything until she presses the
button that was always there.

── A PAGE, NOT ONE CLICK ───────────────────────────────────────────────────

Outlook and Gmail fire requests at the buttons in an email to check them for
safety. A one-click skip is a child moved by software nobody pressed, so
/skip-stage/<token> names the child and the stage and waits, exactly as
/post-call, /call, /application-call and /send-interest-link all do.

It reads the LIVE stage, which is what makes an old email safe to press: a
leader opening a three-week-old inquiry notice is shown where the child is
today. Konnor Broyld's tour was read off a calendar three weeks after the
fact on 13 October. Letters outlive the state they describe.

── THE LETTER IS SENT, NOT JUST QUEUED ─────────────────────────────────────

Queueing is not sending. Nothing leaves admissions_communication_queue until
processCommunicationQueue runs, and the only things that run it on a schedule
are the queue crons, which exist to cover 7am and 8am Eastern rather than to
answer a button somebody is standing in front of. A letter that arrives at the
next cron is not "should then receive the next email", so the queue is drained
on the press with the same service-role client
/api/admissions/process-communications already uses on its cron path.
