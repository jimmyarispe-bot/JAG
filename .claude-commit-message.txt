Jimmy, 16 October 2026, in five words. Every button in every letter carried a
second paragraph under it - "Or paste this into your browser:" and then the
whole URL again, in grey, 12px. It is gone.

He had already struck every one of them out of the flow document on 15
October, marking each DELETE by hand. This is the same instruction applied to
the letters themselves, which is where a parent actually meets them.

── THE REASON IT EXISTED WAS WRITTEN DOWN AND IT WAS BACKWARDS ─────────────

  "The naked URL stays, smaller, underneath. A button is not a link to
   somebody whose client strips tables, and a letter that becomes unusable in
   Outlook is worse than an ugly one."

Outlook renders tables. Tables are the standard way to lay an email out
PRECISELY BECAUSE Outlook's engine handles them and handles little else. The
fallback was guarding the least likely failure and charging every letter a
repeated URL for it.

THE LINK IS NOT LOST. The <a> sits INSIDE the table cell rather than being
replaced by it, so a button is still a link to anything that can read an
anchor. The test asserts that, not just the absence.

WHERE IT COST MOST is where he noticed it: 561's Virtual assessment letter
carries two Google appointment-schedule links of about 120 characters each,
so the two buttons he had just approved arrived under 240 characters of grey
URL. The mockup he called "exactly what we need" did not have them.

── TWO CONSTANTS WENT WITH IT ──────────────────────────────────────────────

MUTED and LINK_INK existed only for that paragraph and had no other reader.
LINK_INK's own note said "the variable stays, because the next colour may not
be so forgiving" - true while something used it, and AN UNUSED CONST IS A LINT
FAILURE THAT FAILS A VERCEL BUILD WHILE TSC STAYS GREEN. Its contrast
reasoning is kept in a comment where it stands, for the day that paragraph
comes back.

── THE TEST FIXTURE WAS TWO MIGRATIONS STALE AND NOBODY NOTICED ────────────

letter-html-buttons.test.ts holds a copy of inquiry_staff_alert's body
labelled "verbatim from the live row". It was not. 557 appended the Skip next
stage paragraph and 558 changed the call to action to Jimmy's words, and the
constant still carried neither. Every assertion passed the whole time: a
fixture that is wrong is still internally consistent, it has just stopped
