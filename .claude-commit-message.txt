Help text written for parents was never shown to them

Jimmy renamed "Date of Birth" to "Birthdate" through the new form builder and
noticed the sentence beneath it had gone. It had not gone. Comparing v22 and
v23 of the published definition returned exactly one difference - the label -
so the help text was still there, and had simply never been rendered.

QuestionField builds one shared label block and it printed the label alone.
Every question type that uses it - text, date, email, phone, number, the
school picker, the program picker - dropped helpText silently. Three branches
(file upload, multiselect, currency) each wrote their own help paragraph,
which is why some help text appeared on the form and some did not: it
depended on the question's type, not on anything anybody decided. The boolean
and consent branch, where the help text is the part explaining what is being
consented to, showed nothing either.

The help now belongs to the label, so it appears under whatever kind of
question carries it. The currency branch's duplicate paragraph is removed.

WHAT THIS COST: every sentence written to guide a parent through a text or
date question has been invisible for as long as the form has existed. Nobody
would report it - a parent cannot miss a sentence they have never seen, and
the person who wrote it has no reason to check.

The diagnostic that settled it is committed:
supabase/diagnostics/what_the_publish_changed_2026_09_26.sql - it diffs the
two newest versions field by field, and is the right first move whenever a
form change appears to have done something it was not asked to do.
