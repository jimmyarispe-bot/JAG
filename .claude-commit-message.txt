PAYSHEET CHANGE 4 of 6, from Jimmy on 8 October 2026:

    "this function only for me - add ability to enter a different pay amount
     than what the jag figured"

and, asked who: him AND Danni, keeping to the standing rule that only those
two see anything to do with money.

Migrations 519 and 520 are the database half. This is the screen.

IT SITS BESIDE THE COMPUTED FIGURE AND NEVER REPLACES IT

override_total_cents is its own column next to frozen_total_cents. The
computed figure keeps being computed and keeps being shown, and the panel
prints both:

    $550.00 is being paid, not the $520.00 the JAG worked out.
    guest cover combined into her own class, agreed

A paysheet read in March has to answer two different questions - what did
the platform work out, and what did a person decide to pay. One number
cannot answer both. Collapse them and an override becomes indistinguishable
from a fault in the rate table, which is the worst thing that can happen to
a pay record nobody can reconstruct.

THIS IS THE ONLY ACTION IN EITHER FILE THAT TAKES A FIGURE

payroll-actions.ts refuses numbers from the browser on purpose, and says so
at the top: approving reloads the week server-side and freezes what IT
computes, so the money is produced by one implementation of pricing with
tests behind it.

The override is the opposite case by definition. It exists precisely because
a person disagrees with that implementation. A figure nobody typed is not an
override, it is the total again.

DOLLARS IN, CENTS STORED. A screen that asks for cents is a screen that will
one day pay somebody a hundred times what was meant. A stray $ or comma is
forgiven; anything else is refused by name, so "1,2 00" cannot quietly
become 1200. The multiply is rounded rather than truncated - 550.29 is
55028.999... in floating point, and a truncation there is a cent a teacher
never sees and nobody can find.

A REASON IS REQUIRED TO SET ONE, AND NOT TO CLEAR ONE

Going back to what the JAG computed is not a decision that needs defending.
Departing from it is. It is a free sentence and not a dropdown, because the
useful reasons are never on the list - Renee Tracewell's week is the
example, where the honest answer is "guest cover combined into her own
class", which is not a category.

Enforced in the function as well as the screen. A disabled button is a
courtesy to the person using it, not a rule.

WHO IS ALLOWED IS NOT DECIDED HERE

set_teacher_week_override checks may_administer_teacher_pay() inside itself,
the same predicate approve_teacher_week and reopen_teacher_week use, because
security definer runs past row-level security and a check around a function
is not a check.

Migration 519 wrote its own role list there - has_role('FOUNDER') or
has_role('EXECUTIVE_DIRECTOR') - which was correct and was a SECOND
definition of a question this build already answered. 520 removed it. One
answer to who may touch a teacher's pay, and changing it changes all three
functions at once.
