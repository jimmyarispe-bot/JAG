── 2. THE BOARD HAD NO EXIT ────────────────────────────────────────────────

  "also add in the dropdown under the last step - Move to Quiet and
   whenever that is selected move student to Quiet Students page"

Taking Declined, Decision Needed and Alumni out of the card dropdown that
morning left a board a child could enter and never leave. One option now
sits under the thirteenth step.

"MOVE TO QUIET" IS A VERB; the thirteen above it are states. It sets
waitlisted, so they land in Decision Needed — because that is what is
true the moment a leader parks a family: nothing is moving and somebody
still has to decide. Declined and Alumni are judgements, made on the
Quiet Students page once the child is out of the way of the families
still in play.

ONE STAGE, TWO WORDS, DELIBERATELY — the card says what she is doing, the
column says where they arrived. Two names for one thing has been a fault
all week; this is the exception and it is written down so nobody quietly
"fixes" it.

ALLOWED_TRANSITIONS now lets every stage reach waitlisted. Declined and
not_returning always could; waitlisted could only be reached from
shadow_day_completed, which left Quiet Students with three columns and
two ways in. Nothing is enforced by that table — transitionCaseStage never
consults it — so this makes the case page's suggestions honest rather
than unlocking anything.
