feat(teacher): a teacher can say she missed a class

PAYSHEET CHANGE 3 of 6, from Jimmy on 8 October 2026:

    "teachers need to identify/select which classes they missed each day"

WHAT WAS WRONG

A teacher's week is built from what she ADDS. If she did not teach a class
she simply never adds it, or removes it. So the JAG holds no record that a
class was supposed to happen and did not, and a missed class is
indistinguishable from one nobody ever scheduled.

Which means nobody can see a gap in cover without asking her, a colleague
who stood in has nothing to point at, and the week reads as complete when it
is not. An absence recorded is an absence somebody can act on. An absence
that is only a missing row is one nobody will ever find.

WHAT THIS ADDS

Migration 518 puts two columns on teacher_class_entries:

    missed       boolean not null default false
    missed_note  text

and a partial index on the missed rows only, which is the one the planner
wants for "what was missed this week" and a fraction of the size of a full
one.

The row STAYS, at its real day and hour. That is the whole point: deleting
the entry is what happens today and is exactly the behaviour being fixed.

THE PRICING IS DECIDED BEFORE THE RATE IS CONSULTED

week-view.ts returns zero for a missed line BEFORE it looks at the roster.

This matters more than it reads. The children stay ticked on a missed class
on purpose — she marked who was due, and whoever covered it needs that list.
But a roster is exactly what the pricing reads. Leaving the decision to
classPay would pay her for a morning she was not there, because the class
still has five children on it.

A missed line also carries no pricing PROBLEM. A missed class with nobody on
it would otherwise shout "could not be priced" at a teacher who had just
told us plainly that she was not there.

WHY NOT instructional_sessions.session_status

setClassHeldAction already writes 'cancelled' there, and that is a different
fact: the SCHOOL'S record of whether a session happened, on a table the
teacher's own week does not read. The week screen is built from
teacher_class_entries.

Writing one and reading the other is how two systems come to disagree about
the same morning — which is the lesson of this week twice over: Danni's
calendar invisible for five days because the JAG held
danni.treu@theacademyfl.org while Google answered to
danni.treu@theacademyway.org, and Cassandra Manghum's name spelled two ways
in two tables.

ON THE SCREEN

The button says what it will DO — "I missed this", and "I did teach this" to
undo it — rather than labelling the state. A button marked "Missed" on a
class she taught is ambiguous at a glance on a Friday evening.

The amount is struck through, the row explains itself in a line, and the
register is HIDDEN rather than removed: the children stay on the row for
whoever covered it, but a teacher who was not there has no business taking
an attendance she did not see.

Nothing is writable on a submitted week, as before.
