A choice can be absent, rather than offered and then refused

Jimmy, 27 September: a Georgia resident taking Virtual or HS classes may use
their GA Special Needs scholarship, but cannot apply for GA GOAL or
Academy-Based - "those options should not be available to them".

The form engine could not express that. Visibility was per section and per
question, and this is the same question asked of the same family with a
shorter list. The two workarounds available were a second, near-identical
scholarships question, or offering all four and refusing on submit. The first
splits the answer across two keys that every downstream rule then has to know
about; the second shows a parent something they cannot have and teaches them
the form's choices are not to be trusted.

So an option may now carry its own visibleWhen, and resolveStaticOptions
takes the answers and applies it. ONE function, which is the point: the
renderer omits what it omits and the validator refuses what it omits, because
both ask the same question of the same code. An option hidden on screen but
accepted on submit is a rule anyone who can post a form can ignore.

Passing no answers returns every choice, which is what a caller describing
the form wants rather than one filling it in - the admin forms screen, a
report on what the form asks.

pruneAnswersForHiddenOptions is the companion, and the form is broken without
it: a family who ticks GA GOAL and then changes programme still has ga_goal
in their answers, and would be refused at submit against a box they can no
longer see. The renderer now prunes on every change rather than only when the
campus question moves, because a choice can now be withdrawn by any answer
its rule reads.

Twelve tests. No form changes in this commit - this is the mechanism the
residency work needs, shipped on its own so it can be wrong in isolation.
