A parent could not submit the application she had just filled in

Lisa Roy opened her invitation link this morning, completed Jayden's whole
application, signed it, pressed submit and was told:

    Unable to submit
    Unknown question: invitation_token. Please try again.

She had done nothing wrong and there was nothing she could do. She wrote in to
say so: "I reviewed the application and completed all of the required fields,
and I did not see any indication that information was missing."

WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED. The invited route posts the family's token in the same
body as their answers - that is how the server knows whose application this
is - and `formDataToInterestValues` sweeps every field in that body into
`values`. INTEREST_FORM_METADATA_KEYS is the only thing separating a form
field from a question, and `invitation_token` was not on it. So the validator
looked at the family's own credential, found no question by that name, and
reported it as a question Lisa had invented.

It is metadata in the truest sense: it identifies the submitter and is never
stored as an answer. Unlike `source` beside it, it IS read from the browser,
deliberately - the link is the credential, and carrying it in the body is the
whole mechanism. It is verified in apply-link/token.ts before anything is
written, and this change does not touch that.

WHY NOTHING CAUGHT IT. The invited route is the only route that carries a
token. Every test, and every submission through the public form, exercises a
body that has no token in it, so no existing test could have failed. The new
test stands in for the parent who did: it asserts the token is accepted, that
it never lands in the stored answers, and - the part that matters as much -
that a field which really is unknown is still refused. The guard is a list,
not a pass-through, and it should stay one.
