THE CODE

onRosterOn now answers three questions rather than two. The third is new: DOES
THIS CHILD COME ON THAT WEEKDAY, AND AT THAT HOUR. Campus children attend Monday
to Thursday and Friday mornings, and are gone by one o'clock. 13 of the 41
sections start at or after 13:00; across 13 Fridays that is 169 classes where
counting them would overpay. The class runs five days - this is a fact about a
child, so it lives on the enrolment, not the section.

computeClassPay now excludes cancelled and no_show sessions. Jimmy: "if a
teacher is absent or has technical issues and does not hold the class then they
are not paid for that class." The default is paid; not holding it is the
exception, recorded rather than left silent. NOTE: nothing in the JAG can mark a
session cancelled yet. The rule is enforced and unusable until attendance
exists.

rateOn takes the teacher and prefers their own rate over the course's, matching
workRateOn, so a course-wide rise cannot silently cancel an individual
agreement.

A missing rate still returns null rather than zero, and a teacher who cannot be
priced is NAMED as skipped. A zero-pound row in a pay run is indistinguishable
from a teacher who taught nothing, and that is how somebody goes unpaid quietly.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO

No pay period has been computed and no money has moved. There is no screen for
entering a non-class claim, no approval flow, and no way to mark a class
cancelled. The rates and the rules are in place; the doors are not built.
