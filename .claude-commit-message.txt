fix(admissions): an archived lead is not chased

THE NIGHTLY CHASE and THE INTEREST-MEETING CHASE.

WHAT WAS HAPPENING

Migration 228 added archived_at / archived_by / archived_reason to
admissions_leads so a lead could be taken off a list without erasing a
child's record. Its own comment names the case it was built for:

    "the only way to remove one was a hard delete, which is why the test
     rows and the duplicate entries are still sitting in the pipeline"

Sixteen files under src honour that column. The two jobs that chase
families every night were not among them.

    parent-reminders.ts:462   .not("automation_started_at", "is", null)
                              and nothing else

    chase/run.ts:345          .gte("created_at", since).limit(1000)
                              and nothing else

So a lead archived from the board carried on being chased, every night,
indefinitely — the one thing archiving exists to stop.

HOW IT SURFACED

On 7 October Logan Astor's mother submitted the public interest form
three times, because the form was taking fifty seconds to answer her
(fixed in ab5ae152). The Academy Virtual received three lead rows for
one boy.

Archiving the two extra rows is the obvious cleanup, and on its own it
would have changed nothing: Heather Brown would have gone on being
chased every night about two children who do not exist. Migration 512
had to reach in and clear automation_started_at on those two leads by
hand to stop it.

This is the general fix, so the next archive does not need one.

WHAT IS AND IS NOT CHANGED

Two sweeps gain `.is("archived_at", null)`. Nothing else.

The per-lead lookups elsewhere in these files — .eq("id", leadId) —
are deliberately untouched. Reaching an archived lead by its id is
somebody acting on purpose, and that is allowed. A SWEEP that picks up
an archived lead is the job forgetting it was put away.
