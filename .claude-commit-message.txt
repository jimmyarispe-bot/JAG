the leader is not here

Out of office, built to Jimmy's correction: "i didn't say nothing moves, i
said the skip to next step needs a human."

While a school leader's calendar is marked out of office for a whole working
day, a new inquiry at her campus gets the interest meeting link sent to the
family the same night instead of waiting for her. It calls
sendInterestMeetingLink - the same function her own button calls, with the
same token - so the machine does exactly what her press does and nothing
more. No stage is skipped. No gate is answered. No judgement is made.

The three stages that ARE a judgement - the tour/shadow-day invite and both
decision gates - wait for her however long she is away.

WHAT HAD BEEN THROWN AWAY SINCE 2 OCTOBER. chase/calendar.ts drops an event
with no dateTime and an event with no attendees. An out-of-office block is
normally both, and nothing in src/ had ever read Google's eventType field. So
the scan has read every leader's absence every night for a fortnight and
discarded all of them. The collector now sits above both guards and reads
eventType === "outOfOffice", never the title: the calendars are full of
events called "Office" and "Lunch Break".

TWO GUARDS, BOTH FROM THE LIVE PIPELINE. Nothing older than 14 days is
touched - Virtual holds 18 children at Interest Meeting Held untouched since
26 August, and a family that has heard nothing for seven weeks needs a person,
not a cron. And a Monday-to-Friday absence acts once per child, enforced by a
unique index rather than a check in the code.

Heather is the booking contact for HS and Virtual both, so one absence reaches
two campuses. That falls out of school_admissions_contacts rather than being
special-cased.

WHILE YOU WERE OUT (564). The letter she gets on the first working day back,
naming every family written to in her absence. Jimmy's words, subject cut to
four by him. Sent outside the admissions queue deliberately:
admissions_communication_queue.lead_id is NOT NULL, and anchoring a letter
that names five families to one child would put four other families' names,
grades and phone numbers on that child's record. Rendered through
renderLetterHtml so it carries the same chrome as every other letter.

THE SHIP GATE CAUGHT THIS, CORRECTLY. 564 put three tokens in a letter body
that MERGE_FIELDS had never heard of - the migration-294 failure, which once
put three families two days from reading "{{student_first_name}}'s place".
All three are now registered and valued.

Also 562: the HS application asks whether the student should attend
Structured Literacy or Math, Jimmy's sentence unchanged, HS only, not
required. Interest form v41 published, v40 archived.

Migrations 562, 563 and 564 applied. 26 new unit tests, all passing; the
absence walk looked only backwards in the first draft and would have reported
"day 1 of 1" on every day of a week.

Not proven live: the code is not deployed and the site is unreachable from
the build environment. The live proof is a real Out of office block on a real
calendar, then the dry run.
