# Revenue Recognition

## Bases

- **Cash** — recognize on collection (`kind: cash`)  
- **Accrual** — recognize / defer independently of cash  

## Entry kinds

`deferred` · `recognized` · `contract` · `grant` · `subscription` · `cash`

Invoice metadata tracks `deferredAmount` and `recognizedAmount`.  
`recognitionSummary` rolls up totals for operational dashboards (not financial statements).
