# 02 — Financial Reasoning

## Analysis pack

Trend · historical · comparative · ratio · liquidity · profitability · operational · working capital · capital structure — produced by `runFinancialAnalysis`.

## EBITDA

| Variant | Definition |
|---------|------------|
| EBITDA | Operating income + depreciation + amortization |
| Adjusted EBITDA | EBITDA + audited adjustments |
| Normalized EBITDA | EBITDA + recurring adjustments |

Adjustment kinds: recurring · one_time · owner_compensation · non_operating · depreciation · amortization · other. Every adjustment has rationale + evidence refs.

## Quality of Earnings

Scores revenue/expense quality, recurring share, customer/vendor concentration, risk factors, normalization notes.

## Cash runway

Current/available cash, monthly & forecast burn, runway months, best/expected/worst sensitivity.

## Valuation

Income · market multiple · EBITDA multiple · DCF placeholder · asset approach · scenario comparisons · history.

## Recommendations

Every recommendation includes supporting evidence, confidence, financial impact, assumptions, and ≥1 alternative. AI never posts journals.
