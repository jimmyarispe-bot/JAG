## 1. Vision Statement

> **By 2035, The JAG™ shall be the world's most trusted mastery-based learning enterprise — where every learner's journey is evidence-led, every family is partnered, every educator is equipped, and knowledge authored once transforms lives globally.**

The JAG will not be measured by software features alone, but by **demonstrable human outcomes**: literate, numerate, life-ready graduates who carry verifiable competency records into college, career, and community.
