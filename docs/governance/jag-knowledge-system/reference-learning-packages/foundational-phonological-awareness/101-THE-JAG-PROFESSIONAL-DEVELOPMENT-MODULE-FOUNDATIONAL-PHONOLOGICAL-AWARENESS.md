## 5. Unit Content & Reflection Questions

### Unit 1 — PA Foundations & Academy Way Alignment

**Content summary:** Definition of PA; oral vs. print; mastery-not-age; JAG asset consumption model.

**Reflection questions:**

1. How does The Academy Way "open entry with evidence-based placement" change your assumptions about who needs PA instruction?  
2. Why must PA evidence remain oral during initial competency stages?  
3. What is the difference between a learning profile and a diagnostic label in your own words?  
4. How does Document 98 establish the gold standard for future competency libraries?

**Practice:** Write a 3-sentence plain-language explanation of PA for a parent.

---

### Unit 2 — Competency Sequence & Placement

**Content summary:** 8 groups, 24 competencies, prerequisite graph, recursive review.

**Reflection questions:**

1. Why must sentence segmentation precede syllable blending in the Document 98 sequence?  
2. When might a learner be on PA-011 (rhyme) concurrently with PA-007 (syllable segment)?  
3. What evidence would you require before advancing from PA-014 to PA-016?  
4. What does PA-024 validate that individual competency L3 does not?

**Practice activity 1 — Placement simulation:**

- Review learner profile: age 8, reads slowly, guesses at words, claps syllables inconsistently (2/5 probe).  
- Assign starting competency with rationale.  
- List two prerequisite checks before instruction begins.

---

### Unit 3 — Explicit Instruction & Session Design

**Content summary:** Document 99 session template; OG principles; one task type per micro-block.

**Reflection questions:**

1. Why are 10–15 minute PA bursts recommended over 30-minute blocks?  
2. What is the 70/30 new-to-review ratio and how do you implement it?  
3. Name three common instructional mistakes from Document 99 §12 and how you avoid them.  
4. How do you use nonsense words appropriately in blending tasks?

**Practice activity 2 — Session plan:**

- Write a 12-minute session plan for PA-007 (segment 2-syllable words).  
- Include: preview, review competency, model, guided practice, formative check, evidence plan.  
- Align to Document 22 playbook sections.

---

### Unit 4 — Assessment, Evidence & Progress Monitoring

**Content summary:** Probe administration; PA-L3-bundle; progress decision rules.

**Reflection questions:**

1. What is the minimum evidence bundle for L3 on any PA competency?  
2. Why is parent log alone insufficient for mastery validation?  
3. What action follows three flat weekly probes?  
4. When is a retention probe required?

**Practice activity 3 — Evidence documentation:**

- Given probe scores (3/5, 4/5, 4/5, 4/5 over 4 weeks) and one observation checklist pass — is learner ready for advance? Explain.  
- Draft KEE evidence entries (conceptual — type, source, competency_key, confidence).

---

### Unit 5 — Differentiation, EF & Accommodations

**Content summary:** Bands; profile dimensions; accommodations vs. lowered criteria.

**Reflection questions:**

1. Give an example of a valid accommodation and an invalid "accommodation" for PA.  
2. How do you differentiate for a multilingual learner without reducing rigor?  
3. What EF supports help a child with attention variability during PA?  
4. When do you trigger 1:1 grouping?

**Case study 1 — Maria:**

- Grade 2, bilingual (Spanish/English), strong rhyme in Spanish, syllable segment 2/5 in English, fatigues after 7 minutes.  
- **Tasks:** (a) Assign competency entry point. (b) Design 2 accommodations. (c) One home activity from Document 100. (d) Family message draft.

---

### Unit 6 — AI Coach, Family Partnership & Fidelity

**Content summary:** Rule keys; prohibited AI actions; family communication.

**Reflection questions:**

1. List three AI actions that require human gates for PA.  
2. How do you respond when AI suggests Tier 2 intervention?  
3. What do you share — and not share — at a parent conference?  
4. How does Document 102 support fidelity monitoring?

**Case study 2 — James:**

- Age 7, flat probes 3 weeks on PA-014, AI flags `rhyme confusion` pattern incorrectly (actually blend collapse).  
- **Tasks:** (a) Validate or reject AI suggestion with evidence. (b) Select reteach strategy. (c) Draft teacher response to parent who asks "Does my child have dyslexia?"

## 7. Knowledge Checks

### Check 1 — Foundations (Unit 1)

1. Phonological awareness operates at which unit sizes? *(Words, syllables, onset-rime — not individual phonemes as primary focus)*
2. True/False: Letter knowledge is required for PA evidence. *(False)*
3. What mastery level means all success criteria met? *(L3 Proficient)*

### Check 2 — Sequence (Unit 2)

1. Which competency is the library capstone? *(PA-024)*
2. Name one prerequisite for PA-014. *(PA-005 and PA-012 — either acceptable with full credit for both)*
3. What progression type includes cumulative review every session? *(Recursive)*

### Check 3 — Instruction (Unit 3)

1. Recommended PA session length? *(10–15 minutes)*
2. Minimum probe threshold for stage proficiency? *(4/5)*
3. Name one common instructional mistake. *(Any from Doc 99 §12)*

### Check 4 — Assessment (Unit 4)

1. Minimum evidence types for L3? *(2)*
2. Maximum parent-only evidence confidence? *(0.55)*
3. What trigger initiates Tier 2 review? *(Flat probe 3 sessions)*

### Check 5 — Integration (Unit 6)

1. Can AI auto-declare L3 mastery? *(No)*
2. Name one permitted parent home activity. *(Any from Doc 100 §3)*
3. What rule key governs handoff suggestions? *(sl.aic.pa.advance)*

**Passing:** ≥ 80% per check · all checks required for completion.

## 8. Case Studies (Facilitator Notes)

### Case A — Older Struggling Reader

**Profile:** Grade 4, reads at Grade 1 level, never received explicit PA, embarrassed about "baby games."

**Discussion:** Age-neutral placement; dignity-preserving language; dosage without condescension; path to PA-024.

### Case B — Advanced Preschooler

**Profile:** Age 4, strong rhyme, claps syllables, attempts onset-rime.

**Discussion:** Evidence-based acceleration within graph; don't skip PA-001 without evidence; capstone still required.

### Case C — Network Fidelity Audit

**Profile:** Coach reviews 5 teachers; 2 skip review segment; 1 uses letter cards during PA-001.

**Discussion:** Doc 102 rubric domains; coaching conversation scripts; remediation plan.
