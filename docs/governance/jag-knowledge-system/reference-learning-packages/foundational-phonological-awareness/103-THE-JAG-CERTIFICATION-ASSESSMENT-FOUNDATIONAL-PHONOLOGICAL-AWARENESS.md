## 1. Charter

The **Foundational Phonological Awareness Certification** validates that an educator can **deliver, assess, differentiate, document, and coach** PA instruction aligned to Document 98 at fidelity.

## 5. Assessment Blueprint

### 5.1 Component Overview

| Component | Type | Weight | Duration |
|-----------|------|--------|----------|
| **A** | Written Knowledge Examination | 30% | 90 min |
| **B** | Case Analysis Portfolio | 20% | Take-home — 7 days |
| **C** | Live Practical Demonstration | 30% | 45 min |
| **D** | Observation & Fidelity Evidence | 20% | Pre-submitted |

### 5.2 Component A — Written Knowledge Examination

**Item count blueprint:** 60 items total

| Domain | Multiple Choice | Short Answer | Scenario |
|--------|-----------------|--------------|----------|
| D1 | 6 | 1 | 1 |
| D2 | 10 | 2 | 2 |
| D3 | 12 | 2 | 2 |
| D4 | 6 | 1 | 1 |
| D5 | 8 | 1 | 1 |
| D6 | 4 | 1 | 0 |
| D7 | 4 | 0 | 0 |

**Item rules:**

- All scenarios use fictional learners — no real student data  
- No Wilson proprietary terminology as scored content  
- OG principles referenced as instructional category only  
- Source alignment: Document 98 success criteria, Document 99, Document 51  

**Sample item types (blueprint — not operational items):**

- *MC:* Which competency precedes onset-rime blending?  
- *SA:* List three evidence types valid for PA L3 bundle.  
- *Scenario:* Learner scores 2/5 on PA-007 third week — describe next steps.  

### 5.3 Component B — Case Analysis Portfolio

**Deliverable:** Written analysis (1,500–2,000 words) + evidence plan

| Case | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Case 1** | Multilingual learner — placement and home partnership |
| **Case 2** | Older struggling reader — dignity and dosage |
| **Case 3** | Flat probe trend — Tier decision and documentation |

**Scoring dimensions:**

| Dimension | Weight within B |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Competency placement accuracy | 30% |
| Evidence plan completeness | 25% |
| Differentiation & accommodations | 25% |
| Family communication (Doc 100 alignment) | 20% |

### 5.4 Component C — Live Practical Demonstration

**Format:** Candidate delivers 12-minute PA session to 2–4 learners or validated simulation panel

| Segment | Duration | Evaluated |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Preview & criteria | 1 min | D1, D2/D3 |
| Review | 3 min | D2 |
| Instruction | 5 min | D2 or D3 (assigned competency) |
| Formative check | 2 min | D5 |
| Debrief with evaluator | 1 min | D6 |

**Assigned competencies (rotating forms):**

- Form A: PA-007 (segment 2-syllable)  
- Form B: PA-014 (blend onset-rime)  
- Form C: PA-011 (identify rhyme)  

**Evaluator:** Certified PA cert holder or JAG-approved coach  
**Rubric:** Document 102 coach instrument — must score ≥ 4.0 on Domains 1–4

### 5.5 Component D — Observation & Fidelity Evidence

| Evidence | Requirement |
|----------|-------------|
| Coach observation | Document 102 — signed within 90 days |
| Probe log sample | 4 weeks de-identified PA probe data |
| KEE evidence sample | 3 anonymized evidence bundles |
| Family communication sample | 1 de-identified parent message |
