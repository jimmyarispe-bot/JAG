## 1. Definition

**Vocabulary** encompasses **knowing word meanings, usage, and relationships** — oral vocabulary and reading vocabulary — depth and breadth for comprehension and expression.

**JAG definition ID:** `SL-CL-VOC-DEF-001`

---

## 2. Purpose

Build word knowledge for comprehension, writing, and academic success across domains.

---

## 3. Educational Importance

Vocabulary is the strongest comprehension correlate alongside fluency; Tier 2 academic vocabulary critical for content area reading.
