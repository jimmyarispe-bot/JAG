## 1. Definition

**Orthographic Mapping (OM)** is the **process of storing written words in long-term memory through secure bonding of phonemes, graphemes, and meaning** — building fluent word recognition without rote memorization alone.

**JAG definition ID:** `SL-CL-OM-DEF-001`

---

## 2. Purpose

Establish permanent word representations for high-frequency and pattern-regular words; reduce cognitive load during reading.

---

## 3. Educational Importance

OM explains how skilled readers recognize words instantly — through letter-sound-meaning connections, not visual whole-word memorization without phonology.

---

## 4. Developmental Progression

| Stage | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **1** | Map decodable HF words (CVC, taught patterns) |
| **2** | Map words with taught digraphs/patterns |
| **3** | Map irregular words **with explanation** (e.g., heart words — phoneme exception noted) |
| **4** | Spaced retrieval — word recognition fluency |
| **5** | Handoff | Stable OM → Automaticity (95) / Fluency (90) |

## Part II — Enhancement Profiles

### A. Cognitive Science Profile (Doc 52)

Retrieval Very High; LTM **Very High**; WM Moderate (mapping episode); Semantic activation Moderate (meaning link).

**Instructional implications:** Map only after decode success on pattern; spaced retrieval (Doc 18); explain irregularities — never flashcard without analysis.

### B. Brain Development Profile (Doc 53)

Orthographic lexicon in left ventral temporal cortex; consolidation during sleep; mapping strengthens with successful decoding attempts.

### C. Instructional Decision Model (Doc 54)

**When:** Accurate decode on word pattern ≥ 3 exposures. **When NOT:** OM drills before decoding pathway established. **Spacing:** SIE spaced review slots.

### D. AI Reasoning Profile (Doc 55)

**Mastery:** Retention probe ≥ 4/5 at 2-week spacing; recognition latency improving. **Rule keys:** `sl.aic.om.practice.spacing`, `.practice.retrieval`
