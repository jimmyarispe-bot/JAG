## 1. Definition

**Fluency** is **reading accurately, at appropriate rate, and with expression (prosody)** — freeing cognitive resources for comprehension.

**JAG definition ID:** `SL-CL-FLU-DEF-001`

---

## 2. Purpose

Transition from accurate decoding to efficient reading; enable comprehension allocation.

---

## 3. Educational Importance

Fluency is the bridge between word-level literacy and meaning-making; weak fluency caps comprehension even with strong decoding knowledge.

---

## 4. Developmental Progression

| Stage | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **1** | Accurate slow reading — prioritize accuracy |
| **2** | Increased rate on controlled text |
| **3** | Automatic word recognition clusters |
| **4** | Prosody — phrasing, punctuation, expression |
| **5** | Grade-level fluency on appropriate text band |
| **6** | Handoff | Fluent on band → Comprehension (93) intensive |

## Part II — Enhancement Profiles

### A. Cognitive Science Profile (Doc 52)

Retrieval Very High; Attention High; WM Moderate (comprehension emerging); Automaticity central.

**Instructional implications:** Repeated reading; paired reading; model fluent reading; never sacrifice accuracy for speed.

### B. Brain Development Profile (Doc 53)

Automaticity reduces prefrontal load; fluent reading networks integrate visual, phonological, semantic systems.

### C. Instructional Decision Model (Doc 54)

**When:** Decoding accuracy ≥ 95% on instructional text. **When NOT:** Speed drills before accuracy. **ORF monitoring:** Weekly when active target.

### D. AI Reasoning Profile (Doc 55)

**Mastery:** ORF within band; prosody rubric ≥ 3/4. **Rule keys:** `sl.aic.fluency.assess`, `.schedule.spacing`, `.practice.repeated_reading`
