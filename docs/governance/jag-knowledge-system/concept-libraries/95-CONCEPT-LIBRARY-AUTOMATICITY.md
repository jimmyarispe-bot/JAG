JAG IP · Automaticity through retrieval practice — not speed-at-all-costs.

---

## 1. Definition

**Automaticity** is **performing decoding and word recognition with minimal conscious effort** — rapid, accurate retrieval freeing resources for comprehension.

**JAG definition ID:** `SL-CL-AUT-DEF-001`

---

## 2. Purpose

Reduce cognitive load of word reading; enable fluency and comprehension allocation.

---

## 3. Educational Importance

Automaticity is the mechanism underlying fluent reading; without it, comprehension suffers regardless of decode knowledge.

---

## 4. Developmental Progression

| Stage | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **1** | Accurate slow — no speed pressure |
| **2** | Timed practice on mastered patterns |
| **3** | Retrieval practice — spaced intervals |
| **4** | Automatic word list clusters |
| **5** | Integrated automaticity in connected text |
| **6** | Sustained automaticity maintenance |

## Part II — Enhancement Profiles

### A. Cognitive Science Profile (Doc 52)

Retrieval **Very High**; Procedural automaticity **Very High**; WM decreasing load as automaticity builds; Attention Moderate once automatic.

**Instructional implications:** Spaced retrieval (Doc 18); daily brief drills on **mastered** content only; interleave patterns.

### B. Brain Development Profile (Doc 53)

Procedural consolidation; reduced PFC activation as skills automatize; hippocampal to cortical shift for word storage.

### C. Instructional Decision Model (Doc 54)

**When:** Accuracy ≥ 95% before speed/automaticity focus. **Never:** Timed drills on unmastered patterns. **Spacing:** SIE daily retrieval slots.

### D. AI Reasoning Profile (Doc 55)

**Rule keys:** `sl.aic.automaticity.practice.retrieval`, `.practice.spacing`, `.assess.timed_probe`
