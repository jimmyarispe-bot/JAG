## 1. Definition

**Written Expression** is **communicating ideas in writing with accuracy, clarity, and purpose** — integrating encoding, vocabulary, syntax, and discourse knowledge.

**JAG definition ID:** `SL-CL-WE-DEF-001`

---

## 2. Purpose

Demonstrate integrated literacy; enable academic and life communication through writing.

---

## 3. Educational Importance

Writing reveals integration of all literacy subskills; portfolio and transcript evidence (Docs 10, 11).

---

## 4. Developmental Progression

| Stage | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **1** | Sentence writing — complete, punctuated |
| **2** | Varied sentence types |
| **3** | Paragraph — topic, details, closure |
| **4** | Short composition — narrative/informational |
| **5** | Revision and editing |
| **6** | Handoff | Proficient → LitLab composition / Transfer (97) |

## Part II — Enhancement Profiles

### A. Cognitive Science Profile (Doc 52)

WM **Very High**; EF **Very High** (planning, revising); Motor Moderate–High; Semantic High; Metacognition High.

**Instructional implications:** Scaffold planning; separate encoding from composition when dysgraphia; rubric-based feedback.

### B. Brain Development Profile (Doc 53)

Writing integrates motor, language, and executive networks; development extended through secondary years.

### C. Instructional Decision Model (Doc 54)

**When:** Encoding and sentence syntax emerging. **Extended blocks:** Writing workshop time. **Accommodations:** Keyboard, speech-to-text per profile.
