# JAG Success Intelligence
### Self-Healing Customer Success & Autonomous Support Intelligence

Version: 1.0
Status: Planned
Owner: JAG Platform
Priority: Critical

---

# Vision

Every organization that uses JAG should receive enterprise-grade customer support without hiring a customer support department.

Users should never wonder:

• Why isn't this working?
• Who do I contact?
• Is this a bug?
• Did someone break something?
• What do I do next?

JAG should immediately recognize the issue, investigate it, guide the user, verify the outcome, learn from the interaction, and continuously improve.

---

# Purpose

JAG Success Intelligence exists to solve subscriber problems.

NOT internal JAG engineering problems.

NOT developer debugging.

NOT platform administration.

Its only responsibility is helping customers successfully use JAG.

Examples include:

• Login problems
• Password reset failures
• Missing payments
• Tuition posting delays
• Scheduling problems
• Parent portal issues
• Employee portal issues
• Missing reports
• Permission problems
• Notifications not arriving
• Email delivery issues
• Mobile app issues
• Data synchronization issues
• Printing problems
• Upload failures
• Calendar issues

---
