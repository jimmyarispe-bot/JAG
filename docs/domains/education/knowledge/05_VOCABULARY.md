# 05 — Education Vocabulary

Canonical terminology in `EDUCATION_VOCABULARY`.

Goals:

- Preferred terms (e.g. **Student**, not “pupil” as canonical)  
- Stable `education.term.*` identifiers  
- Optional aliases for discovery (non-canonical)  
- Alignment with entity / capability naming  

Validator enforces unique vocabulary ids and unique preferred terms (case-insensitive).
