One column would have been wrong at half the campuses. 448 gives schools
contracting_legal_name and signatory_line, each transcribed from that campus's
own contract - the opening paragraph for one, the "Agreed to and Accepted by"
block for the other. Nothing is derived from the campus name, because the
relationship between a campus and its LLC is not a pattern.

NULL IS THE REFUSAL. A school with no contracting entity recorded cannot have a
contract rendered for it. Any campus added later starts NULL and stays that way
until somebody states the entity. A contract naming the wrong LLC is worse than
no contract.
