# Economic Intelligence Architecture

## Composition
Economic is the terminal intelligence module after Impact. Soft result types derive a baseline without importing upstream implementations. `types.ts` and `contracts.ts` remain leaf-oriented.

## Economic Lens
Every recommendation answers: economic forces, supporting evidence, confidence, organizational areas impacted, financial implications, operational implications, strategic options, and scenarios to monitor.

## Pipeline
Baseline derivation → 18 area assessments → trends → forecasts → scenarios → analysis suite → scoring → dashboards and reports → reasoning/projection → persistence.

## Closed learning loop
Economic evidence creates contributions for Market, Revenue, Funding, Operations, Opportunity, Executive Decision, and Predictive Intelligence. Economic consumes upstream results and publishes contributions; it does not mutate upstream domain packages.
