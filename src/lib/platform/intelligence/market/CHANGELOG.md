# Changelog — Market Intelligence

## 0.1.0 — Sprint 043

- Initial release of Market Intelligence (`market`).
- 12 capability submodules: industry, competitive, market size (TAM/SAM/SOM),
  pricing, customer demand, demographic, geographic expansion, economic trend,
  technology trend, partnership, mergers & acquisitions, and white space
  opportunity intelligence.
- Market Health Score, Competitive Position Score, Expansion Opportunity Score,
  and Market Risk Score (inverted pressure semantics).
- Competitive / Expansion / Trend dashboards, Executive Market Brief, and a
  signals suite covering all `MARKET_SIGNAL_KINDS`.
- 8-field market recommendation lens on every recommendation.
- Soft integrations from Knowledge, Document, Legal Compliance & Risk, Revenue,
  Funding, Customer, Business Model, Operations, Predictive, and Opportunity.
- Factory: `createMarketIntelligence()` returning `MarketStack`.
