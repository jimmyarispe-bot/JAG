# Impact Intelligence Architecture

## Composition
Impact is the terminal intelligence module after Innovation. Soft result types derive a baseline without importing upstream implementations. `types.ts` and `contracts.ts` remain leaf-oriented.

## Impact Lens
Every recommendation exposes outcome achieved, supporting evidence, baseline, change, confidence, attribution, improved goals, and the next improvement.

## Pipeline
Baseline derivation → 12 area assessments → measurement suite → outcomes → ROI/SROI → scoring → dashboards and reports → reasoning/projection → persistence.

## Closed learning loop
Measured lessons create Knowledge drafts, Organizational Improvement actions, Executive Decision signals, and Innovation experiment signals. Impact consumes upstream results and publishes contributions; it does not mutate upstream domain packages.
